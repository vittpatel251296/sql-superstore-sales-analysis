# Business Insights – Superstore Sales Analysis

## 1. Revenue Overview
- Total revenue: 3050
- Total profit: 915
- Average order value: 203.33

## 2. Regional Performance
- Top-performing region by sales: West
- Lowest-performing region by sales: East
- Observation: West region shows strong revenue and the highest average order value.

## 3. Product Category Performance
- Highest-revenue category: Furniture
- Highest-margin category: All Categories have equal 30% margin
- Observation: Furniture category, despite having the highest revenue, the profit margin generated by all categories is the same.

## 4. Customer Insights
- Top 3 customers by sales: Bob Johnson - 900, Charlie Wilson - 900, John Doe - 575
- Observation: A small group of customers contributes a disproportionate share of revenue.

## 5. Daily Sales Trend
- Line chart from daily sales data shows spikes in the middle. It is not a steady graph. There is only 1 order per day.

## 6. Recommendations
Based on this sample data, I would recommend:
- Focus on selling more products each day.
- Build a loyalty program for top customers.
- Investigate regions with low sales.
- Stock more furniture category products.
